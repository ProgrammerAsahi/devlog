# devlog

A public development log of my journey into AI infrastructure and inference optimization — recording the route as I walk it, and keeping a window open to the outside world.

## Why write in public

This log records my learning and growth path, and it is a window through which the outside world can see who I am and how I work. Private notes are easy to abandon; public writing is a commitment.

## The machine behind this log

My RTX 4090 is part of a top-spec PC I built in late 2023 after saving up for a long time. Its original purpose was simple: playing games at max settings with top-tier graphics. But in the AI era, it has become the most valuable free compute resource I own — and putting it to work on AI infra and inference learning feels exactly right.
